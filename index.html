About

We believe dance should be accessible.

Kinetiq Dance Company is a contemporary dance collective based in San Diego. We exist to give dancers a space to grow, create, and perform — without the barriers that usually come with it.

Our seasons run in cycles, with weekly rehearsals, collaborative choreography, and a final performance. No one is turned away due to lack of funds. If cost is a barrier, reach out.

— Josephine Venegas, Founder
